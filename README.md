## 1. Project Overview

NIVORA is a low-cost, lightweight and portable passive milk chilling and storage can designed for small-scale dairy farmers.

The system uses thermal insulation and reusable ice-gel packs to help maintain pre-cooled milk during transportation without continuous external power.

It also includes temperature monitoring and hygienic milk dispensing.

---

## 2. Problem

Small-scale dairy farmers transport milk from farms to collection centres where established chilling infrastructure may not be immediately available.

During transportation, milk temperature can rise and affect milk quality.

NIVORA addresses this first-mile cold-chain challenge through passive cooling, thermal insulation and temperature monitoring.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

NIVORA combines:

- HDPE outer body
- PU foam thermal insulation
- Aluminium reflective layer
- Reusable ice-gel packs
- Food-grade SS304 inner tank
- Digital temperature sensor
- LCD temperature display
- Insulated lid
- Hygienic dispensing system

The design is intended to reduce heat gain and help maintain pre-cooled milk during transportation.

---

## 6. Layered Design

The NIVORA structure consists of multiple thermal and protective layers.

### Outer Layer
HDPE provides lightweight protection and structural support.

### Insulation Layer
PU foam reduces heat transfer between the surroundings and the milk chamber.

### Reflective Layer
An aluminium layer helps reduce radiant heat transfer.

### Cooling Layer
Reusable gel packs provide passive cooling around the inner tank.

### Inner Tank
The SS304 tank provides a hygienic milk-contact surface.

---

## 7. Temperature Monitoring

A waterproof digital temperature sensor is positioned inside the milk chamber.

The sensor is connected to a microcontroller and LCD display.

The system provides real-time temperature information to the user during transportation.
